### Classification Taxonomy
- **SUPPORTED / VERIFIED:** Mathematically and hydrologically proven from raw timestamped observations, identical feature pipelines, and verified dependence-aware inference.
- **QUALIFIED:** Scientifically valid within strictly defined boundaries; generalizability or causal interpretations are bounded.
- **UNSUPPORTED / SUPERSEDED:** Refuted by empirical data, confounded by experimental design artifacts, or failing statistical significance.
- **UNRESOLVED:** Cannot be independently verified due to absence of authentic operational records (e.g., gate discharge telemetry or statutory disaster dispatch logs).
