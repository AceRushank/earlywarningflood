Data Sources & Scientific Provenance

1. CWC / Maharashtra WRD Telemetry (Station 006-SW-MAHA)

Hourly verified river stage observations (m MSL), rate of rise (m/hr). Warning Level 354.00 m, Danger Level 354.80 m, FRL 355.00 m. Coverage: 2024 (1,154 records) & 2026 (2,568 records). Zero synthetic values.

2. ISRO / MOSDAC INSAT-3DS HEM Satellite (V01R00)

4 km half-hourly rain rate / hourly depth (2,869 native pixels) across 51,768 km² Upper Godavari basin. Archive: July 31 – August 5, 2024 (275 granules). Model D trained on satellite features.

3. IMD 0.25° Daily Gridded Precipitation (Pune)

71 grid cells spanning Upper Godavari basin. Maintained at native daily resolution (mm/day); never synthetically downscaled to hourly.

4. Open-Meteo Multi-Model NWP API (ECMWF / ICON / GFS)

Hourly numerical weather prediction forecasts for station area and 7 verified interior catchment points. Operational live input; not satellite observations.

5. JRC Global Surface Water v1.4 (1984–2021) & HydroBASINS Lev08

Historical water occurrence and recurrence patterns (30m). Delineated reach corridor (1,231.5 km²; sub-basins 4081058170 & 4081060220). Water occurrence represents persistence, not verified flood extent.

6. Sentinel-1 SAR Flood Inundation Pipeline

GEE IW VH change-detection workflow (scripts/gee_event_flood_extractor.js). 500 m spatial model is untrained pending local SAR masks (tile N19E077).

Scientific Qualifications & Safeguards: (1) Historical water occurrence is not verified flood extent. (2) Spatial 500m flood model is untrained pending authentic SAR labels; zero synthetic labels manufactured. (3) Stale telemetry (>3h) blocks operational stage predictions. (4) Weather source mismatch (NWP vs satellite) blocks Model D live inference.
